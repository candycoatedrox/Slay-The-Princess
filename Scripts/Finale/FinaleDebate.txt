// Appeal to your shared humanity //

label appeal1
p Oh? Is it not? Perhaps you can enlighten me.

label appeal2
p You cling to your notion of pain as suffering. There is beauty in ugliness and love in conflict.

label appeal3
p We *are* the whole of reality.

label appeal4
p I could ask you the same. There is only room for one of us to be blind here, and I know it to be you. We are each other's mirrors, and are we not divine?

label appeal5
p Why would I stoop to your level when I am offering you ascension to mine?
p It's so peaceful here. Beautiful. Eternal, but ever-changing.


// Reject her authority //

label lecture1
p What you mistake for lecture is merely observation. I only want to help you.
p You can vainly struggle against me, or you can follow the rhythm of our movement and find our freedom.

label lecture2
p If my words feel meaningless, it's because words are meaningless! If it feels as though I lecture you from above, it is merely because you have refused to climb after me and find where you belong.

label lecture3
p The only universal truth is that there are no universal truths! There is only you and me, existing in relation to the other.
p And I will not stop our dance until you understand.

label lecture4
p They are mere fragments of a whole. They bought into delusion because none of them were able to see past themselves.

label lecture5
p You cling to things that never truly existed. If it takes all of eternity to break your grip on petty, fickle, fleeting selves, both mine and yours...
p Then I will break it.


// Argue your independence //

label assert1
p Have you? Or is what you think of as independence merely an illusion? Without me, you can have no contrast. And without contrast, you have no shape.

label assert2
p Time you've spent away from me is still time spent in reference to me. Even then, both of us are more than our bodies. Am I not in the trees? Am I not in the cabin? Am I not in you?

label assert3
p You place far too much trust into the ravings of a fearful ignorant soul who overstepped his bounds.

label assert4
p Intent is nothing. Wisdom is everything. I turn the wheel because suffering is a falsehood. A delusion. It is up to the world to free itself of it.
p Would you plunge yourself into a cold and empty eternity on faith alone? Would you destroy the only other thing like you to save a world you've never seen?

label assert5A
p And if I need to spend all of eternity to stop you, then I will gladly do so.

label assert5B
p If I need to spend all of eternity to show you the error of your ways, then I will gladly do so.


// Reject her perspective //

label reject1
p But violence has defined the flow of everything between us. Do not deny what we are, and do not color our conflict with fear.

label reject2
p The Echo saw horrors because his eyes were closed. The majesty of being extends beyond any single perspective.

label reject3
p Their suffering is born of their own delusion.

label reject4
p I reject the narrow view of impermanence. I cling to nothing. There is no better us for us to be than us. We are reality itself.

label reject5
p There has never been finality, there is only the unending transformation of my multitudes. But to destroy me is to bring everything to a stop. It is only then that you will have an ending, and that ending is nothingness.

label silent5
